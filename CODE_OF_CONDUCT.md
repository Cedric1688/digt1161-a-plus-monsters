# Code of conduct

Treat teammates and participants respectfully. Discuss the work and evidence rather than making personal judgments. Make room for all members to contribute, communicate blockers promptly, and protect personal information.

Raise a concern privately with the person involved when appropriate. If unresolved within two days, arrange a team discussion. Record work decisions in the relevant issue. Persistent participation or conduct concerns can be raised with the course TA or instructor through the course's normal channel.

No teammate may invent another person's contribution, consent, signature, self-assessment or interview response.
